## What a document costs (measured)

Means per document over a random sample of each benchmark (seed 0), read one document at a time by the final pipeline (v1.4; UNIKIE-BENCH by its protocol's readings and zoom re-reading). Calls and tokens: each reply's own usage, counted in the measuring process (`benchmarks/usage_meter.py`). DocuBench and VAREX were measured alone; ExtractBench and LongArray-Extract side by side, and the two UNIKIE-BENCH tracks side by side with two other measurements, so their seconds include waiting for each other. DocQuorum runs open weights on one H100 shared with other accounts' jobs: it pays no price per call, and the seconds are upper bounds. The dollar columns price the measured tokens (extraction model and parser) at the Qwen3.8-27B list price, $0.50 / $3.00 per million input / output tokens (`pricing.json`). Per-document rows: `results/costs/<benchmark>.json`.
